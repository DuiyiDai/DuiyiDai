# Duiyi (Claire) Dai

**Empirical economist & data scientist · Equity Research**

I work in Equity Research at UBS, using alternative data and empirical methods to study company and sector performance across European equities. Previously, I worked at the University of Oxford on computational social science. I hold a PhD in Economics from the University of Birmingham.

My research brings together economics, NLP, large language models and computer vision, with an emphasis on economic uncertainty and reproducible empirical analysis.

## Selected research and software

- **[RobustiPy](https://github.com/RobustiPy/robustipy)** — collaborative Python software for multiverse and specification-curve analysis. Our paper is published in [Patterns](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.patter.2026.101609).
- **[Brexit Uncertainty Index](https://duiyidai.github.io/brexituncertaintyindex/)** — news-based measures of aggregate and topic-specific economic uncertainty. [Website source](https://github.com/DuiyiDai/brexituncertaintyindex).
- **[UK Parliamentary Debates](https://github.com/centre-for-care/hansard)** — collaborative research infrastructure for parliamentary text data.
- **[Visual bias in the Brexit referendum](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.euroecorev.2026.105422)** — published in *European Economic Review*, based on a chapter of my PhD thesis.
